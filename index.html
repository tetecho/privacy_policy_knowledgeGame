Played on one device. Nothing shared.

Bariq is a pass-and-play party game for Face to Face and Imposter. Scores, teams and answers never leave your phone. This page lists every single thing that does leave it.

Pass-and-play, one device No accounts, no sign-in No GPS · No contacts · No biometrics Free, supported by ads
📱 One device, no network play Both game modes are played on a single phone or tablet by people sitting together. Bariq has no online multiplayer and no servers hosting live games.
📥 Content is downloaded Questions, categories and words are fetched from our servers so the game always has a fresh library to play with.
⚙️ Settings are remembered Your player name, language and theme are stored server-side under an anonymous device key, so they come back if you reinstall the app.
🛡️ No ad SDK profiles of your game data Google AdMob handles advertising separately. Your scores, answers and teammates' names are never shared with ad networks.

01 Information We Collect

Bariq does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your real identity. Because the game is played on a single shared device, we have no way to know who is playing or which real person typed a given nickname. The information below is limited to what the app needs to work.

1.1 Information you provide

Data Why we hold it
Player name / nickname Shown on screen during play so players can tell teams apart. Your nickname is also saved to our servers so it is restored after you reinstall the app. It is not tied to a real identity and is never shown to anyone outside your own device.
Language and theme Your language and dark/light theme choice, saved so the app opens the way you left it on a new install.
Sound, haptic and reminder settings Your notification and reminder preferences. These are applied on your device and are not sent to our servers.

1.2 Information generated automatically

Data Why we hold it
Anonymous device key Android's ANDROID_ID or Apple's vendor identifier, which your operating system generates for apps. We use it as the key to your saved settings. It contains no name, email or account, and we cannot use it to identify you as a person.
Random player ID A random identifier generated on your device the first time you open the app. It tags your saved settings and your play records so the two can be linked to each other. It is not derived from anything about you.
Play activity When you play a question in Face to Face or a word in Imposter, the identifier of that question or word is sent along with your random player ID. We use this to see which content players actually use, so we can retire content nobody plays and spot broken entries. It does not include your answers, score, or who won.
Push notification token If you allow notifications, we store a delivery token for your app installation so reminders can be sent to you. It is not a contact list, and we cannot read your messages or other notifications.
Device and app details Device model, operating system version, app version and platform, submitted with your notification token to help us diagnose delivery problems on specific devices.
Request metadata Like any web service, our hosting provider records the IP address, timestamp and requested URL of each request in order to serve it and protect against abuse.

None of these identifiers are linked to your name, address, phone number or any account, and we do not use them to build a profile of you.

1.3 What we do NOT collect

  • Real name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise or approximate location (GPS or network-derived)
  • Contacts or address books
  • Calendar data
  • Photos, camera images, or files from your device
  • Microphone audio or recordings
  • Biometric data such as face or fingerprint templates
  • Social network credentials
  • Health or fitness information
  • Payment card numbers
  • Your game answers, scores, teams or results
  • Advertising or analytics profiles built by us

We do not operate our own analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The only advertising and delivery SDKs in the app are listed in Section 5.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Download and update the question, category and word library.
  • Restore your player name, language and theme after a reinstall.
  • Understand which game content is used, so we can improve or retire it.
  • Send the reminders you have opted into.
  • Keep the service available and fix technical problems.
  • Detect and block abuse, cheating and automated traffic.
  • Serve and measure the advertising that funds the app.
◆ We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your game activity, scores or device key with advertisers, and we do not use your play records to target advertising.

03 Data Stored on Your Device

Most of Bariq lives entirely on your device. The following is kept in the app's private storage and is not transmitted to us:

  • Team names, the current game session, and its scores
  • Every answer you give, and the correct answers used to score the round
  • Statistics, streaks and achievements
  • Sound, haptic and reminder settings
  • Downloaded copies of questions, categories and words
  • Onboarding state

Your player name, language, theme and anonymous player ID are stored both on your device and on our servers. That is the only information duplicated off-device.

Deleting local data

You can remove everything stored on your device at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. Neither action removes the copy held on our servers — see Section 11 to have that deleted.

Two operating-system behaviours are worth knowing: on Android, system backup may restore your settings to the same device after a reinstall; on iOS, the system keystore can retain your anonymous player ID after the app is uninstalled. In both cases the restored value is the random identifier, not anything you typed into a public field.

04 What Leaves Your Device

Bariq talks to one service, our content and settings API. This is the complete list of requests the app can make, with what each one contains.

Endpoint Method Sent
/category get Nothing. Fetches the list of question categories.
/jimsin get Nothing. Fetches the Face to Face question library.
/blanko get Nothing. Fetches the Imposter word library.
/player-state/{deviceId} get Your anonymous device key, as the address of the record.
/player-state/{deviceId} put Device key, random player ID, player name, language, theme, and whether onboarding was completed.
/jimsin/mark post Random player ID and the ID of the question you played. Sent after the round ends, never with your answer.
/blanko/mark post Random player ID and the ID of the word you played. Sent after the round ends, never with your score.
/push-token post Push delivery token, platform, device model, OS version and app version. Sent only if you grant notification permission.

Every request is made over HTTPS. Requests that fail are discarded silently and retried later; nothing is queued on any third-party service.

✓ Never sent: team names, scores, answers, correct answers, round results, photos, contacts, location, or anything typed into the game during a round. That information exists only on the device and is lost when you clear the app.

The app checks whether a connection is available before it will start a game, because it needs a working connection to fetch content and to serve advertising. This check is performed locally by your operating system and is not reported to us or to any third party.

Our content and settings API is hosted on Vercel. Vercel processes the standard technical information needed to serve and secure any web request, including IP addresses and timestamps.

5.3 Expo — Push notification delivery

Bariq is built with Expo. Reminder notifications are delivered through Expo's push notification service, which acts as a courier: it receives your delivery token and forwards the notification to your device. It does not gain access to your notification content or contacts.

Google Play and the Apple App Store process information associated with installing, updating and using the application through their platforms. If a future version of Bariq offers purchases, payment information will be handled by the relevant app store and never by us.

Bariq does not currently offer in-app purchases.

06 Advertising

Bariq is free and funded by advertising, using interstitial and rewarded ad formats. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

  • No game data is shared: your scores, answers, player name and the identity of the people you play with are not provided to AdMob. Only the advertising identifier and standard technical data described above are involved.
  • Advertising identifiers: Google AdMob may process the device Advertising ID and technical information needed to serve, measure and prevent abuse of advertising.
  • Personalisation depends on your settings: Bariq does not request Google's "non-personalised ads" flag, so whether ads are personalised depends on your device's ad settings, the platform, and your region. Where required by law, Google's own consent process applies.
  • Device controls: depending on your device and operating system version, you can reset or restrict the advertising identifier, and opt out of personalised ads, from your device's privacy or ads settings.

Advertising and privacy choices vary by country or region and are subject to applicable law and platform requirements.

07 Children's Privacy

Bariq is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13, or the higher minimum age required by applicable local law.

  • We do not knowingly request a child's real-world identity for gameplay.
  • We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children.
  • Because the game is played on a shared device and we hold no account or contact details, we cannot verify a player's age. We rely on users not providing identifying information as a player name.
  • If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner prohibited by applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

Parents or guardians may contact us using Section 14.

08 Data Retention

Different types of information are kept for different periods, depending on why they are held.

  • Data on your device remains there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
  • Your settings record — device key, random player ID, player name, language and theme — is kept until you ask us to delete it, or until the service is discontinued.
  • Play activity records — which questions and words were played — are kept while that content remains in the game library, so we can judge what to keep.
  • Push notification tokens are kept until you remove the app, revoke notification permission, or ask us to delete them.
  • Server logs containing IP addresses and request timestamps may be kept for a limited period for security, debugging and abuse prevention.
  • Advertising information is retained and managed by Google according to Google's own policies.

09 Data Security

  • App data is held in the operating system's private application storage, which is not readable by other apps.
  • Identity values such as your anonymous player ID are additionally stored in the hardware-backed system keystore (iOS Keychain or Android encrypted shared preferences), which is isolated from the rest of the app's storage.
  • All communication with our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access to our backend services is limited to the systems needed to operate Bariq.
  • Bariq holds no payment card information, as it offers no purchases.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information Bariq holds.

10 International Data Transfers

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, the relevant provider may rely on mechanisms such as adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), or other legally recognised transfer mechanisms.

11 Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live and which laws apply to you, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request the information held about you.
  • Correct — request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Delete — request deletion of your server-side record.
  • Restrict / Object — request limits on certain processing.
  • Port — request your information in a portable format where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent.
  • Complain — contact your local data-protection authority.

These rights may arise under laws including the GDPR, UK GDPR, CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA, and other applicable privacy laws.

How to exercise your rights

Email us using Section 14. Tell us the app you are using, your player name and roughly when you last played, and we will locate the matching record. You do not need to provide any identity document to make a request.

On request we will delete your server-side record, which means removing your device key, random player ID, player name, language and theme, and the play-activity entries associated with your player ID. Your device will still hold a local copy until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Because some information is processed by third parties, certain requests — for example anything relating to advertising held by Google — must be handled directly with those providers using the controls described in Section 6.

◆ California (CPRA): depending on the circumstances, California residents may have rights relating to access, deletion, correction, and certain forms of advertising-related processing. Bariq does not sell personal information.
◆ Nevada: Nevada residents may have rights relating to the sale of personal information under applicable law. Bariq does not sell personal information.

12 Do Not Track

Bariq is a mobile application and does not use web browser cookies as a website would. As a result, the app does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals.

Advertising preferences are instead managed through the advertising settings and privacy controls provided by your device, operating system, app store, or the relevant advertising services.
