01 Information We Collect

Bariq is designed to work without an account and without your real identity. We collect only the minimum needed to run the game and its two modes — Face to Face (a head-to-head trivia battle) and Imposter (BLANKO).

1.1 Information you provide directly

DataPurpose
Player name / nicknameShown to other players in Imposter mode and used for in-game greetings. Stored only on your device.
Game preferencesLanguage, theme (dark/light), and sound or haptic settings you choose inside the App.
Purchase decisionsSelecting or declining one-time purchases — removing ads or unlocking the Face to Face game forever.

1.2 Information handled automatically

DataPurpose
In-game progress & statsXP, level, achievements, streaks, daily-challenge results and session records. These are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to us.
RevenueCat App User IDA random, anonymous identifier assigned to your installation by our purchase provider. Used only to verify purchases and entitlements.
Advertising ID (Google)The device advertising identifier used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads. See Section 6.
Transaction & entitlement recordsProcessed by RevenueCat and the app stores to verify purchases, entitlements, and restorations.
Standard technical dataOS, app version, device model, and error/crash logs transmitted by the app stores and ad SDKs.

The RevenueCat App User ID and the Google Advertising ID are separate identifiers with separate purposes — they are never merged into a single profile by Bariq.

1.3 What we do NOT collect

  • Real name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise location (GPS or network)
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, or microphone access
  • Biometric data
  • Social network credentials
  • Any health or financial information

Bariq runs no analytics or tracking SDK of its own. All third-party SDKs are disclosed in Section 5.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate Bariq — starting, saving, and restoring game sessions.
  • Personalize your experience (language, theme, name, preferences).
  • Track in-game progress, XP, achievements, and daily challenges on your device.
  • Process, deliver, and restore in-app purchases.
  • Serve advertising (see Section 6).
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and prevent fraud or abuse.
◆We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use your data to advertise to you outside the App's own advertising.

03 Local Storage (On-Device Data)

Most of your data lives only on your device in the app's private, sandboxed storage and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your player name and game settings
  • Game progress, XP, achievements, streaks, and daily-challenge state
  • Saved challenge sessions and result history
  • Cached quiz questions and offline-mode status
  • Onboarding completion state
  • Local purchase records (an on-device copy used for offline unlocks)

You can erase all of this at any time using the "Reset App" feature in Settings, clearing the app's data in your device settings, or uninstalling the app.

04 The Quiz Server & Content Sync

Bariq downloads its quiz content — categories, Face to Face questions, Imposter words, and Challenge questions — from a content API hosted by Vercel.

The App only downloads content from this API and does not upload player or game data to it. No player identifier, profile data, or game results are sent to the content API. The requests are plain GET downloads and may include standard web request metadata, such as the requester's IP address and request timestamps, which may be processed by the hosting provider as part of providing the service.

  • If a request fails (for example, when offline), the App simply falls back to content already saved on your device.
✓Bottom line: the quiz server only serves quiz content to your app — it never receives player or game data.

05 Third-Party Services

The App integrates the service providers below. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

5.1 RevenueCat — In-app purchases & entitlements

We use RevenueCat to manage purchases and entitlements. It assigns your installation a random, anonymous App User ID, processes transactions, verifies receipts with the app stores, and tracks active unlocks.

5.2 Google AdMob — Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads in the free version. AdMob may process the device Advertising ID, IP-based general area, and app usage data to serve and measure ads, under Google's own policies. The App is configured to request non-personalized ads only (see Section 6).

👉 Google Privacy Policy

5.3 App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store process billing and device data when you install, purchase, or restore products, under their own policies.

5.4 Vercel — Content API hosting

The quiz content API described in Section 4 is hosted by Vercel, which processes standard web-request metadata as part of hosting.

👉 Vercel Privacy Policy

06 Advertising

The free version is supported by ads (interstitial and rewarded).

  • Non-personalized only: Bariq configures its ad requests with requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly. Because we do not serve personalized ads, the App does not run its own consent banner.
  • Ad identifiers: Google may still process the Advertising ID and IP-based information to serve and measure ads. You can reset or opt out of the Advertising ID anytime in your device settings (e.g., Settings → Privacy → Advertising).
  • Removal: a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase disables advertising for your entitlement.

The ad unit identifiers currently configured are Google's test ad units; production units will be set up in AdMob before the full release.

07 In-App Purchases

  • Bariq offers one-time (non-consumable) purchases — Remove Ads and the Face to Face game unlock (product identifiers remove_ads and face_to_face_unlock). There are no subscriptions.
  • Payments are processed entirely by the app stores and RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment card details.
  • Refunds: purchases are subject to the app store's refund policies (Google Play / App Store), not ours.
  • Restore purchases: if you reinstall or change devices, you can restore purchases from your store account. Note that on-demand restoration can be limited by app-store and Google Billing rules; verifying your store account or providing an order ID may be required.

08 Children's Privacy

Bariq's target audience and minimum age are set in each app store listing (see the target-audience configuration in Google Play Console). This policy does not treat Bariq as a child-directed product, and Bariq is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age set in its store listings / local law).

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
  • If we learn that we have collected a child's data in a way not permitted by law, we delete it promptly.
  • Because Bariq serves only non-personalized ads, it does not use child-directed personalized-advertising configurations.

Parents or guardians may contact us at any time via Section 15.

09 Data Retention

  • On-device data is kept until you use Reset App, clear the app's data, or uninstall — then it is deleted.
  • Purchase & entitlement records are retained by RevenueCat and the app stores per their policies and applicable law (tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention requirements).
  • Advertising data is retained by Google AdMob per its retention policy.
  • Content-API requests produce only routine server logs held by the hosting provider for technical operation; they contain no identifiable player content.

10 Data Security

  • On-device data lives in the app's private, sandboxed storage.
  • Purchase validation and receipts are handled by PCI-compliant providers; we never process or store card numbers.
  • All communication with third parties and the content API occurs over encrypted connections (TLS).

No transmission or storage method is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

11 International Data Transfers

Bariq is distributed globally. Providers such as Google and RevenueCat may process data on servers outside your country of residence, including the United States. Where personal data crosses borders, we and our providers rely on the safeguards required by law — including (where applicable) adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), or recognized frameworks such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

12 Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct — fix inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete — request erasure of your data
  • Restrict / Object — limit or object to certain processing
  • Port — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw consent at any time
  • Complain — file a complaint with your local data-protection authority

These rights come from laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK), CPRA (California), LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA (Canada), and the DPDP Act (India).

How to exercise your rights

Contact us at Section 15. Because Bariq has no account system and stores most data locally, we'll typically point you to the Reset App feature, which erases all on-device data immediately. For data held by RevenueCat or Google, use those providers' own channels.

◆California (CPRA): you have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information.
◆Nevada: you may opt out of the "sale" (as defined by Nevada law) of your information. We do not sell your information and have no plans to do so.

13 Do Not Track

Bariq is a mobile application and uses no web tracking cookies, so it does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals. Advertising preferences are managed through your device's ad-identifier settings instead.
